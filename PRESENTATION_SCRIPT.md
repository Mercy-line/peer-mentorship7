### 2. Place of Discovery & Belonging in Tech (1:15 to 2:15)
*(Stay on the **Hero Section**, then scroll slightly to the **4 Badges & 6 Circular Growth Pillars**.)*

"Why *'Where I Found Myself'*? Because KamiLimu is a dream come true for me. It became my place of discovery.

When I first joined, I carried a lot of self-doubt. I used to question whether I truly belonged in tech. Looking around at brilliant minds, it was easy to wonder, *'Am I really supposed to be here?'*

But through KamiLimu, my mindset shifted completely. I realized that **yes, this is where I was meant to be**, because if I wasn't meant to be here, I could not have been in KamiLimu in the first place.

As you can see across my six growth pillars here, from **Public speaking** and **Innovation** to **Resilience**, my **Close circle**, **Appreciation**, and becoming a **Super gal**, every part of who I am today was stretched and reshaped here."

### 3. The Innovation Pillar: From Fear to Favorite (2:15 to 3:30)
*(Action: Scroll down to the **Dusty Mauve Split Banner: "Weuhhh, Sleepless Nights & True Meaning"**.)*

"Let me take you to the chapter that surprised me the most: **The Innovation Pillar**.

At the very beginning, I honestly feared the Innovation pillar. I went into it cautiously, just wanting to see what it entailed. Yet today, I can tell you without hesitation that it ended up being **the best thing that has happened to me so far**.

From learning funnel research, starting broad and narrowing down to a specific part, to problem validation, solution validation, and actually building... *weuhhh!* It has been long nights of no sleep, deep research, and pushing ourselves to make sure what we build brings real meaning.

### 4. Defining Moments: Voice, Resilience & A New Mind (3:30 to 5:15)
*(Action: Scroll down to the **4 Defining Moments & Skills Cards**. Point to each card from left to right as you speak.)*

"Here in the **Defining Moments & Skills** section are four transformations I am proudest of:

**First, my voice, reading, and writing.**
I used to think public speaking was just about communicating your points and getting off the stage as fast as possible. KamiLimu taught me that public speaking is about connecting with the audience and **letting them feel you**. I also used to fear reading long letters and dense research papers, and I feared writing long pieces. Today, I can read any research paper without fear, and while I am still growing as a writer, I am improving with every page.

**Second, unbreakable resilience and focus.**
Before KamiLimu, if I traveled for 4 hours, I needed a whole day of sleep before I could come back to the world. KamiLimu changed that completely. I have been traveling **8 to 9 hours** and still showing up active the very next day. I never knew my body and mind could hold that kind of resilience. Even my daily habits changed: I reduced watching TikTok and Instagram, became drawn to long videos, and through our Saturday sessions, I learned I can concentrate deeply for hours.

**Third, my best year and the art of appreciation.**
This has truly been my best year. Through the Personal Development track, I learned how to write a CV that **qualifies and quantifies** my impact, how to manage my time, and how to learn complex things through **chunking**. And by checking what fellow mentees create in peer mentorship, I learned **the art of appreciation**, where I can look at a piece of art or someone's work and genuinely *feel what that person was feeling*. I never used to be like that."
